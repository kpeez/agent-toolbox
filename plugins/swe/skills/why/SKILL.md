# Why

Answer from the record, not the code. Read the commits and PR discussions
that introduced the target and each later change. Look at other sources, such
as the tracker, team chat, or project docs, only when that trail is thin.
Cite what the authors wrote. Mark your own inferences as inferences. Say what
the record does not answer.
