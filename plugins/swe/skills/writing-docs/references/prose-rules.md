# Prose rules

Scan the text for each pattern below, then rewrite it. Keep the meaning.

## Words

- **AI vocabulary.** Additionally, crucial, delve, enhance, fostering, garner,
  interplay, intricate, landscape, pivotal, showcase, tapestry, testament,
  underscore, vibrant. Use the plain word.
- **Plain word.** "utilize" and "leverage" become "use", "facilitate" becomes
  "help", "numerous" becomes "many", "in the event that" becomes "if".
- **"is" and "has".** "serves as", "stands as", "boasts", and "features"
  become "is" or "has".
- **Metaphor nouns.** substrate, wedge, vector, locus, nexus, primitive (as a
  noun), surface (as in "API surface"), bedrock, paradigm, north star,
  flywheel, endgame. Use the concrete word.
- **One name per thing.** Pick one term for a concept and repeat it. Do not
  cycle synonyms.
- **Adverbs.** "runs quickly" becomes the number; "significantly improves"
  becomes the measured change. Use a stronger verb.

## Sentences

- **One idea per sentence.** Split a sentence the reader must parse twice.
- **Active voice.** Name the actor: "the loader parses the file", not "the
  file is parsed". Use passive only when the actor is unknown.
- **Mechanism, not feeling.** Write what the thing does, or a number. Cut a
  sentence that could appear unchanged in another project's docs.
- **Whole sentences.** Keep articles and verbs. Spell out arrows, symbols, and
  abbreviations.
- **Literal phrases.** Cut aphorisms, rhetorical fragments, personified code,
  and figurative verbs ("rides along", "stands on").
- **Direct claims.** Write "X" instead of "not just Y, but X". Use the natural
  count of items, not a forced three. List topics instead of a false range
  ("from X to Y").
- **Named sources.** Name the source or cut "experts believe" and "some
  argue".
- **-ing tails.** Cut trailing "highlighting...", "ensuring...",
  "reflecting...".

## Filler

- "In order to" becomes "to". "Due to the fact that" becomes "because". Cut
  "It is important to note that".
- One hedge at most: "may", not "could potentially possibly".
- End on a specific fact or plan, not a generic conclusion.
- Cut chatbot phrases and praise: "I hope this helps", "Let me know if",
  "Great question", "Certainly".

## Format

- No em dashes. Use a period or a comma.
- Use a colon before a list or an example only.
- Bold a lead-in only when the text after it adds new detail. Do not bold
  every name.
- Sentence-case headings. Straight quotes. No decorative emojis.
