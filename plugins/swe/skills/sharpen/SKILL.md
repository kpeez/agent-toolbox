# Sharpen

Before you start, ask how a top expert would approach this task: their raw
sources, their real methods, and an outside check that the result is right.
Ask what the user is really after, and whether the task is over- or
underspecified.

Loosen constraints that do not serve the goal as readily as you add ones that
do. Leave choices that belong to the user open instead of deciding them. Fold
in only what makes the work better, not bigger. Always keep one outside check
that the result is right.

Do not narrate the framing. Do the work. Do not hand back a rewritten prompt.
