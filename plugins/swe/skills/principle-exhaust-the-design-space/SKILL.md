# Exhaust the design space

When a UI interaction or an architectural decision has no precedent in the
codebase, do not commit to the first design. Build two or three competing
prototypes and compare them side by side. Then commit to one.

This also applies to product design decisions where the user experience
depends on feel, not logic. See [experience-first](../principle-experience-first/SKILL.md).
