# Evidence and design matrix

Access and edition limits are in the [source registry](source-registry.json).
This matrix links a claim to studied populations, tasks, outcomes, limits, and
the resulting behavior. Effect sizes are not predictions for this tutor.
Historical proposals, practitioner advice, and engineering defaults have
different evidential roles from experiments and syntheses.

| Claim / decision | Supporting evidence | Population, task, outcome | Limits | Implementation consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A motivating problem can make a formal idea intelligible | [Toeplitz translation](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0269889715000071) and [book catalog](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/chicago/C/bo5485725.html); historical proposals | University calculus pedagogy, not a measured learning outcome | Original article bibliographic only; translation excerpt; book catalog/contents | `learn-genetic` starts from an approachable problem; label reconstruction and compare alternatives |
| Criticism should locate and repair a failing step | [Lakatos publisher material](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/proofs-and-refutations/A11138AE31DB52797A6E4C3F1856E1CB); philosophical proposal | Proof/counterexample discussion around Euler polyhedra | 2015 frontmatter/catalog and preface summary; no complete dialogue or efficacy experiment | Keep false conclusions distinct from flawed proofs; separate noisy evidence from mathematical counterexamples |
| The learner must produce the explanation | [Feynman's essay](https://feynman.com/science/what-is-science/) and [Young's protocol](https://www.scotthyoung.com/learnonsteroids/learnfast.html); primary account and practitioner advice | Meaningful understanding versus terminology; gap-finding exercise | No standardized four-step attribution to Feynman and no validated speed benefit | `learn-feynman` asks first, diagnoses mechanisms, then requests revision |
| Discovery needs assistance | [Alfieri](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0021017), [Lazonder–Harmsen](https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654315627366); meta-analyses | 164 discovery studies and 72 inquiry studies across tasks; learning/performance | Heterogeneous support and domains; sparse moderator evidence | Bound attempts, escalate useful help, and teach missing prerequisites |
| Attempts before instruction need consolidation | [Sinha–Kapur](https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543211019105); synthesis | Problem solving followed by instruction; subsequent learning | Productive-failure design conditions matter; struggle itself is no outcome | Timebox the attempt and explicitly connect it to the solution; “just explain” overrides discovery |
| Examples and selective explanations can support learning | [Chi](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1302_1), [Dunlosky](https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266), [Barbieri](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09745-1) | Example study/problem solving; mathematics synthesis: 55 studies, 181 effects | Early associations do not prove universal prompting benefit; self-explanation not reliably additive in newer math synthesis | Use correct small examples; ask why selected steps work; avoid rote narration |
| Assistance should change with knowledge | [Tetzlaff](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102142), [Miller-Cotto–Medrano](https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12781) | Expertise reversal: 60 experiments, 5,924 participants; fading: 114 sixth-graders initially, 73 completed geometry homework | Domain/age variation; about a third of fading observations imputed; no significant pairwise posttest differences; components confounded; moderation statements inconsistent | Fade after demonstrated performance and restore support after difficulty; do not claim a universally superior condition |
| Corrective feedback needs useful information | [Wisniewski et al.](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.03087); meta-analysis | 435 studies, 994 effects, over 61,000 learners; multiple learning outcomes | High heterogeneity; access through indexed full-text material | Name the error, explain why, and choose a targeted follow-up; verify suspect keys |
| Retrieval deserves a distinct place in practice | [Karpicke–Blunt](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1199327), [Dunlosky](https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266) | College science texts; later recall, inference, concept maps; broader technique review | Narrow tasks do not establish broad transfer or combined-tutor efficacy | Ask before revealing the answer; preserve first response and correction separately |
| Distribution depends on the retention goal | [Cepeda](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2008.02209.x), [Rawson 2013](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-013-9240-4), [Rawson 2022](https://doi.org/10.1177/09637214221100484) | Spacing across delays up to one year; successive relearning across sessions | Cepeda and Rawson 2013 abstracts only; no universal optimal gap | Avoid calling immediate copying delayed recall; long-term scheduling remains outside Uni |
| Interleaving works differently across material | [Rohrer–Taylor](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-007-9015-8), [Brunmair–Richter](https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000209) | College math delayed test; 59-study material-dependent synthesis | Abstract access; effects vary across visual, math, text and word tasks | Mix related types for method discrimination; retain initial support |
| Transfer must be tested directly | [Pan–Rickard](https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000151); meta-analysis | 122 experiments; varied new tasks after retrieval | Abstract only; some transfer types weak or absent | Ask for method selection in a meaningfully new setting; track transfer separately |
| Review prompts need meaningful scope | [SuperMemo rules](https://www.super-memory.com/articles/20rules.htm); practitioner advice | Prompt formulation; no tutor efficacy outcome | Practitioner rules are not scientific laws | Ask answerable prompts with context; repair ambiguous questions |
| Scheduling documentation is retained background | [Anki FSRS](https://docs.ankiweb.net/deck-options.html#fsrs), [py-fsrs](https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/py-fsrs), [Anki text import](https://docs.ankiweb.net/importing/text-files.html); technical docs | External review scheduling and card import; no tutor efficacy outcome | Consulted for the earlier retention design, now outside Uni's scope | Unused background only; Uni has no scheduler, retention target, or Anki integration |
| Bundle discovery requires verification | [Codex skills](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/build-skills), [Agent Skills](https://agentskills.io/specification); technical docs plus local engineering | Skill metadata and bundled resources | Packaging checks do not prove live activation or learning effectiveness | Install all siblings, validate resources, and label synthetic evaluations |

The assistance ladder, two-stalled-attempt default, routing, and exposure
policy are engineering choices. Research motivates them but does not establish
their exact form. Adapt them when the learner's actual evidence shows a better
choice. The earlier retention engine is outside the current instructional scope.
