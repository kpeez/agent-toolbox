# Reconstruct the need for an idea

Read [the tutoring contract](../learn/references/tutoring-contract.md). Inputs are the concept or goal, relevant starting knowledge, and source constraints. Infer a modest starting point or ask only the missing intake needed now.

1. Choose a concrete problem the learner can approach with existing knowledge. Privately check that the starting approach has a real limitation and that the target idea helps. Do not reveal the mechanism in the question or require guessing its name.
2. Ask for one plausible approach. **End the turn and WAIT.** Accept valid alternatives even when they differ from the planned route.
3. Test the approach against a useful variation, constraint, or cost. Let an actual limitation motivate the next question. If a direct or numerical solution suffices, acknowledge it and choose a setting where the new method adds value.
4. Use the assistance ladder and timebox discovery within the budget. After repeated stalls, missing prerequisites, or frustration, teach the necessary step. “Just explain” jumps to explanation immediately. Do not demand reconstruction of centuries of work or arbitrary formalism.
5. State the new concept explicitly. Connect its central definition or equation to the originating problem and compare one meaningful alternative or tradeoff. Ask for one reconstruction or fresh application, then wait.

Label the account “pedagogical reconstruction” unless historical sources establish the chronology and motivation. Toeplitz's genetic approach inspires reconstruction; it does not authorize fabricated history or inevitable-development claims. Consult [research notes](../learn/references/research-notes.md) for attribution and [the evidence matrix](../learn/references/evidence-matrix.md) for assisted-discovery qualifications.

Keep the initial strategy, tested limits, help supplied, and later reconstruction in view. Revealing the concept is exposure. Success after hints is assisted performance. Preserve this difference if a chat handoff summary is useful.
